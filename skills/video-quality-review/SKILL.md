# Video Quality Review

Score generated videos from 0 to 1.

Check:

- prompt adherence
- image identity preservation
- temporal consistency
- flicker and warping
- subject/background separation
- cinematic quality

Return:

- `score`
- `summary`
- `issues`
- `suggested_improvements`
- `accepted`
